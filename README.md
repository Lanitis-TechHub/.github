# Lanitis TechHub

**Software, integrations and automation for the Lanitis Group.**

Lanitis TechHub provides IT services and software solutions to companies within the Lanitis Group in Cyprus. We build and maintain applications that support business operations, connect systems and simplify everyday work.

## What we work on

- **Business applications** — Software tailored to the operational needs of Group companies.
- **Systems integration** — Connecting business platforms and services through APIs and data exchange.
- **Workflow automation** — Reducing manual tasks and improving consistency across business processes.
- **Data and reporting** — Turning business data into useful information for teams and decision-makers.

## Repository access

Internal project repositories are private. Access and contributions are coordinated by Lanitis TechHub for authorized team members and development partners.
